# Download KEEZI

## Light up your piano with KEEZI + KEENEKT

See which key to play next, right above your fingers. KEEZI adds a strip of colored lights above your piano keys, and the KEENEKT USB adapter connects KEEZI to the app on your computer. Pick a favorite song, follow the lights with one hand, then bring in the other when you’re ready.

**[Shop the KEEZI + KEENEKT bundle](https://keezishop.com)**

## Get the free app

Start practicing on your MIDI piano today with falling notes or sheet music. You can add the light-up experience anytime.

- **[Download for Windows](https://github.com/KEEZI-LLC/KEEZI-App-Downloads/releases/latest/download/KEEZI-Windows-Setup.exe)** · For 64-bit Windows PCs (x64). Open the downloaded installer and follow the prompts.
- **[Download for Mac](https://github.com/KEEZI-LLC/KEEZI-App-Downloads/releases/latest/download/KEEZI-Mac.dmg)** · For Intel and Apple silicon Macs. Open the download and drag KEEZI into Applications.

## Play your first song

Connect your MIDI piano, open **Song Library**, and choose a song. Select **Watch and Listen Only** to hear it first, or choose **Right Hand** under **Practice the Melody** to play along. The song waits for you to play each note.
